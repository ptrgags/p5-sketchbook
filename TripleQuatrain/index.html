Triple Quatrain 🧪 (2026-07)

I'm exploring methods to build song structures from scratch. This one is inspired by the [Quatrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quatrain) in poetry and song lyrics. The structure is a set of 4 lines, some of which may rhyme (share the same ending sound)

Here I take that structure and use it in multiple places. The whole song is divided into 4 sections. Each section is divided into 4 lines. Each line is divided into 4 phrases.

This gives a rough layout of the song. It's up to the composer to determine how to interpret it.
