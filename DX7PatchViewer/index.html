DX7 Patch Viewer (2026-08)


Instructions: Load a .syx cartridge file (e.g. exported from Dexed) with the file picker. Then navigate through the voices by clicking the left/right sides of the canvas.

This sketch makes a diagram of FM (frequency modulation) synthesis patches. I wanted to make a layout that shows the parameters in the context of the algorithm diagram. The intent is to make it easier to study patches when learning FM synthesis.

The DX7 envelopes are unusual in that they are described by 4 rates and 4 levels. Here I color each stage a different color to help distinguish them. The colors are purple, blue, light blue, orange, and yellow (always in that order). Note that the orange portion represents the sustain portion. This is a constant level while the piano key is held.

NOTE: I don't know how to compute accurate time values for the envelopes from rates/levels, so this visualization just gives the approximate envelope shape.

The envelope at the bottom right of the screen is the pitch envelope.
